# The Sluder and Bowman Families

A static family history for the Sluder and Bowman families, from Rhea and Hamilton counties in Tennessee to Panama City Beach, Florida. It is kept by Shea, son of Judy Darlene (Sluder) Nelson.

The site is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the published copy:

https://shea-nelson.github.io/sluder-bowman-family/

No public portrait is used here. The Family memories and Family photos sections are left ready for the family to fill, and memories are labeled as memory rather than as a record. Facts are cited in the Sources section. Confirmed facts and still-unconfirmed facts are listed separately. Ralph E. Sluder is labeled as very likely Euva’s husband and Judy’s father, because a marriage record or obituary linking them has not been found. Elijah K. Bowman is listed with the Union 6th Tennessee Mounted Infantry, but his relationship to Emmett Houston Bowman is not treated as proved.

Compiled by the family, 2026.
