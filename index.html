A family history

The Sluder and Bowman Families

From Tennessee to Panama City Beach

Rhea County · Hamilton County · Bay County

Kept by Shea, son of Judy Darlene (Sluder) Nelson

This page collects only what can be sourced, and labels what cannot. No public portrait that can be used here has been found. Find a Grave holds grave photographs; those pages are linked in the sources and the pictures were not copied. This page does not pretend otherwise.

01 — Her daughter’s mother

Judy (Sluder) Nelson

Judy Nelson, 65, of Panama City, Florida, died Sunday, June 21, 2020. That is the wording of the obituary published by Wilson Funeral Home. The notice uses the name Judy Nelson. Her son Shea remembers her as Judy Darlene, and her maiden name as Sluder. The maiden name is confirmed in the obituary by the surnames of her brothers, Gary Sluder and Terry Sluder.

She was survived by her husband, by three sons — among them Shea, who keeps this history — and by those two brothers. A private family service was held on June 21, 2020. In lieu of flowers, the family asked that memorial donations be made to the “American Diabetes Foundations,” as the notice spells it, in her memory.

The obituary does not give a date or place of birth, and this page does not invent one. From her age at death, 65 on June 21, 2020, she was born in 1954 or 1955. Where she was born, and when the family settled at Panama City Beach, remain open questions.

02 — Her mother

Euva Bowman Sluder

Euva Bowman Sluder was born August 2, 1923, and died April 28, 1985, at the age of 61. She is buried at Callaway Cemetery in Callaway, Bay County, Florida, in Section E. The dates are on her Find a Grave memorial and in a 2001 survey of that cemetery contributed to the USGenWeb Archives.

The Social Security Death Index records Euva Sluder with the same birth date, a death in April 1985, and a last residence in ZIP code 32407, Panama City Beach, Bay County, Florida. The index often carries only a month for deaths of that period, and lists April 1, 1985; the gravestone and the cemetery survey give the day as April 28, and that is the date this page uses. Social Security numbers are not printed here.

Her Find a Grave memorial names her parents as Emmett Houston Bowman (1888–1972) and Hattie Goins Bowman (1888–1960), and names four siblings: Basil J. Bowman (1911–1974), Grady Bowman (1913–1930), Pvt. Harlie Chester Bowman (1917–1986), and Vernia Bowman Smith (1921–2001). Family links on Find a Grave are user-contributed. The parent names and those years should be read with that in mind, except where another source, set out below, independently confirms them.

  • DiedApril 28, 1985, age 61
  • BuriedCallaway Cemetery, Callaway, Bay County, Florida, Section E
  • Last residence in the Death IndexZIP 32407, Panama City Beach, Florida

03 — Very likely her father

Ralph E. Sluder

Ralph E. Sluder was born November 22, 1915, and died May 26, 2003, at the age of 87. The Social Security Death Index records a last residence in ZIP code 32408, Lower Grand Lagoon, Bay County, Florida — next door, in effect, to Euva’s last residence at Panama City Beach.

Very likely This page treats Ralph E. Sluder as very likely Euva’s husband and Judy’s father. The surname matches Judy’s maiden name. He and Euva both died as residents of adjoining Bay County communities. The family remembers him in that place. What has not been found is a marriage record, an obituary, or a grave inscription that names them as a couple, or that names him as Judy’s father. Until one of those appears, the relationship is not treated as proved.

No burial place for him has been found for this page. A compiled military service record was not opened here, and this page does not add a branch, rank, or service date that it cannot cite. His middle name, beyond the initial E., is an open question.

  • Last residence in the Death IndexZIP 32408, Lower Grand Lagoon, Bay County, Florida

04 — The Bowman line

Emmett, Hattie, and the earlier question of Elijah

The Bowman side of the family is documented in Rhea County, Tennessee, near Chattanooga, and in neighboring Hamilton County.

A Find a Grave memorial at Lone Mountain Cemetery in Graysville, Rhea County, Tennessee, records Emmett Houston Bowman, March 1, 1888 – October 12, 1972. A memorial in the same cemetery records Hattie Goins Bowman, February 12, 1888 – April 9, 1960. Those two memorials match the names and years linked as Euva’s parents on her own memorial. This page accepts the identification as the best public match, and still notes that the parent link itself is a user-contributed family connection on Find a Grave.

Of the four siblings named on Euva’s memorial, one can be confirmed independently in some detail. A Find a Grave memorial for Pvt. Harlie Chester Bowman, March 27, 1917 – August 14, 1986, stands at Chattanooga National Cemetery in Hamilton County, Tennessee, in plot VV #801. The memorial is titled Private and is marked as a veteran. Burial in a national cemetery is evidence of military service. This page does not add a war or a branch beyond what that memorial itself shows, until a service record is in hand.

The years given on Euva’s memorial for Basil J. Bowman (1911–1974), Grady Bowman (1913–1930), and Vernia Bowman Smith (1921–2001) have not been independently confirmed here.

Unconfirmed An Elijah K. Bowman is listed among men remembered with the Union 6th Tennessee Mounted Infantry on a Military History Online regiment page. That regiment was organized at Chattanooga in 1864 and mustered out at Nashville on June 30, 1865, and spent its short service scouting guerrilla bands in the Cumberland Mountains of Tennessee and North Georgia. What has not been found is a record that ties this Elijah K. Bowman to Emmett Houston Bowman, or that states the exact relationship — grandfather, great-grandfather, or none. Until that link is documented, he is not placed in the confirmed line. He appears on the tree below only as an open question.

09 — Still to confirm

Open questions

These are the facts this page would still like to settle. None of them is filled in by guessing. If you or Judy’s brothers know the answers, they would fill in a great deal.

  1. Ralph’s middle name, beyond the initial E. recorded in the Social Security Death Index.
  2. Where Judy, Gary, and Terry were born.
  3. When the family settled at Panama City Beach.
  4. A marriage record, Bible entry, or obituary that names Ralph E. Sluder as Euva’s husband, or as Judy’s father.
  5. Ralph’s burial place, and whether a marker names his wife or children.
  6. Judy’s exact date of birth, and whether a Florida, Tennessee, or other vital record uses the middle name Darlene.
  7. Census schedules that would show Euva in Emmett and Hattie’s household, and later show Judy in her parents’ household.
  8. Any public record of the family’s remembered stops in the Carolinas or California.
  9. A service record for Harlie Chester Bowman, to confirm the war and branch suggested by his national-cemetery burial as a private.
  10. A record that states the relationship, if any, of Elijah K. Bowman of the 6th Tennessee Mounted Infantry to Emmett Houston Bowman.
  11. Obituaries for Euva (1985) and Ralph (2003), which may still be in the Panama City News Herald or in funeral-home files.
  12. Family photographs, and memories the family is willing to have written down.
